Features and Facilities

Facilities listed here are written plainly so parents know what to expect during a visit.

Smart Classrooms

Selected classrooms use projectors and digital material for diagrams, videos and revision work. The focus stays on teaching first, with technology used only where it improves the lesson.

Computer Laboratory

The computer lab supports basic computer handling, typing practice, internet safety, presentations and beginner programming activities.

Science Practical Work

Students are introduced to practical demonstrations and safe experiments in science subjects so concepts do not remain only on the blackboard.

Library Corner

The reading corner includes textbooks, story books, newspapers and other reading material for guided periods and project research.

Sports Time

Regular sports periods and school competitions give students time for fitness, teamwork and discipline outside the classroom.

Transport Support

Transport details are shared by the office based on the route and availability. Parents can confirm pickup points during admission inquiry.

First Aid

Basic first aid is available on campus, and parents are contacted quickly if a student needs further medical attention.

Safe Campus Routine

Visitors report at the office, classrooms follow attendance routines, and staff supervise movement during school hours.
